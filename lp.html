Scope & assumptions

SCOPE

A lease confines writes to one run’s own folder

A grant in leases is keyed by run_id alone. Whatever a container does under that grant — its log, its artifact, every read of config.json — stays inside /runs/{run_id}/. The lease has no reach past that one folder, which is what makes two different run_ids fully independent: nothing about one run’s lease, retry, or failure can touch another’s.

STORAGE

/data is shared, and sits outside every lease

/data holds whatever the ETL pipeline downloads and produces. It’s a sibling of /runs/, not a child of any one run’s folder — every etl container reads and writes the same /data, regardless of which run_id its own lease happens to be keyed to.

RETRIES

Every job and ETL step is assumed idempotent

That assumption is what makes an abandoned attempt safe to simply throw away. A superseded container’s writes sit uncommitted on local disk and vanish with it; a retried one reproduces the same result. Losing work in flight costs time — never correctness.
